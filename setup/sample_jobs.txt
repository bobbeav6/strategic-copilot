This is both a disciplined scaling mandate and a high-upside growth opportunity, an ideal challenge for a CRO who thrives in complex enterprise environments and can move seamlessly between sales execution, segmentation strategy, partner leverage, and organizational leadership. The CRO will be empowered with full ownership across Sales, Business Development, Customer Success, Revenue Operations and Marketing to build forecasting rigor, activate hyperscaler and global consultancy partnerships, lead the transition toward a scalable consumption-based model, and institutionalize repeatable growth frameworks.

 
Salary range between 350,000 and 450,000 USD, based on experience and qualifications.

About The Role

As the VP of GTM Strategy & Ops, you are the operational co-pilot to the SVP of GTM and the central nervous system of our revenue organization. Selling hyperscale AI infrastructure requires a delicate, high-stakes balance: we are marrying massive, capital-intensive infrastructure deployments (featuring long-lead supply chains for GPUs and liquid cooling systems) with volatile, hyper-growth enterprise demand. You have the exciting mandate to take our early operational foundations and engineer them into a highly scalable, world-class GTM engine. Rather than inheriting a rigid legacy playbook, you will have the autonomy to author our definitive operating model. Initially, this means getting highly tactical—optimizing complex capacity monetization models, refining sales territories, and architecting the next evolution of our CRM and data systems. Once these systems are robust and driving predictable revenue velocity, you will step into scale-mode, recruiting and leading specialized leaders across Capacity Planning, Deal Strategy, Competitive Insights, and Revenue Operations to expand the organization globally.

Responsibilities

Architect the Operating Rhythm: Design, implement, and relentlessly iterate on the core GTM operating cadence. This includes establishing rigorous weekly forecasting methodologies, designing impactful Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), and leading the annual strategic planning cycles.
Capacity & Revenue Modeling: Build complex financial and operational models that serve as the connective tissue between our GPU deployment schedules and our sales pipeline. You will ensure we monetize every megawatt of our data centers with maximum efficiency, balancing spot instances with long-term reserved commits.
Build the RevOps & Tech Stack Foundation: Dive deep into our data architecture. You will personally evaluate, select, and implement our core GTM tool stack (CRM, CPQ, forecasting tools), establishing strict pipeline hygiene standards and data definitions.
Cross-Functional Orchestration: Serve as the critical translation layer between Sales, Finance, Engineering, and Supply Chain. You will convert raw physical compute capacity into actionable sales territories and quotas, ensuring the GTM team is always selling what the supply chain can actually deliver.
Strategic Board Reporting: Distill complex operational data into compelling, high-level narratives, partnering directly with the SVP of GTM to prepare Board-level presentations, performance readouts, and strategic investment requests.
